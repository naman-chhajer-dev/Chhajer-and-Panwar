Mr. Arihant Chhajed is a co-founder of the firm. He is a graduate in Bachelor of Commerce and a fellow member of Institute of Chartered Accountants of India with 7 years standing in the profession. He has wide experience in the field of Direct Tax, Audit & Assurance, Bank Audits, Concurrent Audit, and Revenue Audit & Internal Audits. He has vast experience in handling CIT Appeals & assessment proceedings under the Income-tax Act. He has also done the Diploma in Information System Audit (DISA) from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and certificate course on concurrent audit of banks from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.
